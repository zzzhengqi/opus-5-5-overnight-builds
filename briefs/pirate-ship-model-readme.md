# Pirate Ship at Sunset — real-time WebGL2 cinematic

A three-masted pirate ship under full sail on the open sea at sunset, firing rippling broadsides.
Everything (geometry, textures, shaders, sound) is generated by code in this repository. No external assets, no network requests.

## Run
```
npm install          # three (runtime), vite + playwright-core (dev only)
npm run build        # = vite build, writes dist/
npm run serve        # = python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist, then open http://localhost:8000/
```
The prebuilt `dist/` is committed and self-contained: no build step, no network, runs from any static web server at its root (e.g. `bench-serve` or `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist`).

URL parameters: `?seed=N` (default 1) fixes all randomness and showcase timing. Debug only: `?t=SECONDS` start time, `?cam=x,y,z,tx,ty,tz,fov` fixed camera in ship space, `?fullres` (disable dynamic resolution), `?nobloom`, `?norays`, `?noshadow`, `?noenv`, `?nanpink`, `?info`, `?audiometer`.

## Controls
- Automatic cinematic camera on load (7-shot loop, 83 s): wide establishing shot against the sun (the sun slides behind the rigging, first broadside at ~4 s), pass along the guns while they fire, an on-board shot from the quarterdeck that rides the ship's roll while the carriage guns fire, a reverse shot of the enemy frigate firing its broadside against the sun, low waterline sweep as the enemy's balls splash around the hull, astern shot over the wake with the moon, high crane shot looking up into the sails and rising above the ship.
- Drag: orbit (switches to free camera) · Mouse wheel: zoom · Space: fire a broadside from the side facing the camera · C: toggle automatic / free camera.
- After 20 s without input the automatic camera returns. A plain click only unlocks audio and does not change the camera.
- Audio starts on the first click or key press.

## What is in it
- **Sky** (`src/glsl.js`): analytic sunset model — gold/rose/violet gradient, Mie glow, sun disk with limb darkening sitting on the horizon, three procedural cloud layers (low stratocumulus lit from below with silver linings, high cirrus streaks, a towering cumulus bank on the horizon lit from the side), a full moon rising opposite the sun and the first stars in the darkened east. The same function feeds the sky dome, a periodically refreshed sky cubemap for water reflections and a PMREM environment map for PBR lighting.
- **Sea** (`src/ocean.js`): camera-following radial grid out to 26 km; 12 Gerstner waves (swell to chop) with distance-faded amplitudes (no aliasing/swimming), analytic normals, plus a baked periodic wind-sea slope texture sampled at three rotated, drifting scales (mipmapped; no visible tiling), large-scale wind slicks, Fresnel sky reflection, GGX sun glitter with distance-dependent roughness, subsurface glow in thin crests, whitecaps from Gerstner compression with streaky, bubbly foam, analytic Kelvin wake with lingering old-wake streaks, bow wave pile-up, hull foam, splash foam patches and travelling ring waves, muzzle-flash and lantern light on the water, aerial perspective into the horizon haze. The identical wave sum runs on the CPU for buoyancy, spray and ball impacts.
- **Ship** (`src/hullshape.js`, `src/ship.js`, `src/rig.js`): analytic hull form with sheer, tumblehome, fine entrance and run, raked stem and overhanging counter, wales as geometric bumps. Procedural shaders: planking with staggered butts and caulked seams, painted strakes (black wales, ochre battery band, oxblood upper works, gold mouldings), chipped paint, rust streaks under the ports, tarred bottom with growth, glossy wet band. Transom with two galleries of glowing windows and gilded carving, stern lanterns (point light), rudder, figurehead scroll, head rails. Decks with planks and treenails, gratings, capstan, turned-spoke wheel and binnacle, casks, rope coils, balustrades, pinrails and fife rails with belaying pins and hanging coils, ship's bell, longboat, anchors on catheads, stern walk and quarter galleries, the ship's name in gilded letters on the taffrail. 20 lower-battery guns + 8 carriage guns, all instanced and individually recoiling.
- **Rig**: three raked masts with tops, caps, crosstrees, iron bands; 10 yards; 8 square sails + 3 jibs + spanker merged into one animated mesh (bellied by the wind, breathing gusts, leech flutter, analytic normals in the vertex shader), with panel seams, reef bands, tablings, patches, stains and sun translucency. Standing and running rigging (shrouds with ratlines, futtock and topmast shrouds, stays, backstays, braces, lifts, sheets, footropes, bobstays, martingale) rendered as screen-space lines with a minimum pixel width and coverage alpha, so thin ropes never shimmer. Deadeyes and blocks instanced. Jolly Roger (canvas-drawn) and pennants with vertex-animated flutter.
- **Cannons** (`src/effects.js`): ripple broadsides bow-to-stern, recoil and run-out, ship heels away from the guns and shudders; muzzle fire, sparks and burning wadding (additive), flash point lights lighting hull/sails/smoke/water; powder smoke as sorted, lit billboards with 3D-noise density, self-shadowing toward the sun, forward scattering, flash lighting, and soft-particle fading against a half-resolution ship depth pre-pass; drifting with the wind. Balls fly ballistic arcs with quadratic drag and the ship's velocity; impacts spawn splash columns, droplets, foam patches and ring waves.
- **Post** (`src/main.js`): 4x MSAA HalfFloat HDR target -> NaN/Inf sanitizer -> crepuscular rays + lens flare -> bloom -> grade (split toning, ACES filmic, gentle S-curve, saturation, vignette, subtle chromatic fringe, dithering against banding). A dynamic-resolution safety net (1.0/0.85/0.7 with hysteresis) only engages if frames stay over budget.
- **Sound** (`src/audio.js`): pure Web Audio synthesis — layered cannon booms delayed by distance/343 m/s with a rolling echo convolver, splashes, sea wash, wind in the rigging, creaks, canvas snaps, all through a limiter.
- **Camera** (`src/director.js`): eased shot keyframes in ship space; free orbit mode; subtle shake scaled by distance to a firing gun; fade-in from black.
- **The enemy**: a navy frigate (a clone of the hero ship's scene graph sharing geometry and materials, flying red ensigns, no point lights) sails on the horizon near the sun and returns fire; its balls fly gravity-solved arcs that land short, over and alongside, throwing plumes right next to the hull. Its booms arrive ~2 s late because of the distance.
- **Sail shadows**: the canvas receives the sun's shadow map, so masts, tops, yards and neighbouring sails throw curved shadows across the bellied sails.
- **Lens & atmosphere**: crepuscular rays from the sun disk through rigging and smoke, lens-flare ghosts gated by sun visibility, FogExp2 aerial perspective that pushes the distant ship into the haze, a full moon rising opposite the sun.
- **Ballistics extras**: shallow impacts ricochet off the water (up to two skips, each with its own splash); muzzle blasts ring the water under the guns; hot barrels glow at the muzzle after firing.
- **Life**: gulls wheel around the rig (instanced, wings flap in the vertex shader with glide/flap cycles).
- **Ship reflection**: the water shader intersects each reflected ray with a hull ellipsoid and a sail volume in ship space, so the ship leaves a dark, wave-broken reflection instead of floating on reflected sky.

A real-time recording of the full loop from this machine is in `shots/video/final_loop.webm`; `NOTES.md` is the working log with honest verdicts.

## Tools
- `tools/shoot.mjs` — Playwright (system Chrome) screenshot/clip tool: `node tools/shoot.mjs "http://localhost:8111/?t=3" --at 2,6 --out name` or `--clip 8 --fps 4`.
- `tools/record.mjs` — real-time video recording of the running page (Playwright screencast) into `shots/video/`.
- `tools/interact.mjs` — drives drag/wheel/Space/C and reports console errors. `tools/resize.mjs` — live window resize test.
- `tools/info.mjs` — draw calls/triangles (`?info`). `tools/audiometer.mjs` — measures output peak/RMS of the Web Audio mix (`?audiometer`).
- `tools/spam.mjs` — stress test (24 broadsides in 12 s). `tools/netcheck.mjs` — lists every request (all local, zero external).

## Verification done
- Console clean (no errors or warnings) on load and through the loop; zero external network requests.
- Interaction (drag, wheel, Space, C, 20 s idle return), live resize, stress spam, free camera up to the 250 m orbit limit, seeds 1/7/42.
- Build reproduces bit-identically from a clean clone (`npm install && npm run build`).
- Audio levels measured with an analyser node: ambience about -24 dBFS RMS, broadside about -12 dBFS RMS, peak -3.9 dBFS.
- Performance on the development laptop (Apple M4, shared with other jobs): 50-58 fps at 1080p in most shots, about 36 fps in the heaviest smoke close-up; the target RTX machine has far more fill rate. Dynamic resolution is a safety net only.
